HOW IT WORKS

Forecast → Match → Schedule

Our locally trained seasonal/weather model forecasts demand and solar production. A constrained heuristic shifts flexible charging and schedules a battery to reduce projected costs and avoid exceeding the fictional feeder limit where possible.

The optimization is inspectable Python code, not a hidden LLM claim. Real grid safety cannot be inferred from this demo.
MODEL ASSUMPTIONS

What is — and isn't — measured

Profiles, weather, tariffs, battery behavior, connection limits and feeders are synthetic. The official grid sources are provided for planning context, not ingested as real-time capacity.

TRANSPARENCY

Sources & limitations

GridShare is a fictional research prototype. All energy assets, listing values, connected feeder lines, heat zones, prices and savings are simulated. Map tiles may use OpenStreetMap when internet is available.

The official capacity map is linked for separate reference, not embedded as real-time data. The optional published provincial GIS overlay (when online) shows medium-voltage infrastructure only, not live transferable electricity or capacity. We do not claim real-time grid visibility or grid control.
